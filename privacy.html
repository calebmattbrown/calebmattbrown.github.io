Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Church Clips is a personal desktop app that runs on its owner's Mac. This policy explains what it does with information, including information from YouTube.

Church Clips uses YouTube API Services.

By using Church Clips to upload to YouTube, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy (www.google.com/policies/privacy).

What Church Clips accesses

When you connect your YouTube channel, Church Clips asks Google for two permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload the videos you approve, set their title, description and privacy setting, and set their thumbnail.
  • See your YouTube channel (youtube.readonly): only to read your channel's name, ID and picture, so the app can show which channel it is connected to.

Church Clips does not access your email, contacts, watch history, comments, analytics or any other Google or YouTube data.

What is stored, and where

  • The sign-in token from Google and your channel's name, ID and picture link are stored only on your own Mac, in the app's private folder, readable only by your user account.
  • For each uploaded song, the app remembers the new video's ID and link on your Mac, so it doesn't upload the same video twice.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer or to any server other than Google's. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Sharing

Church Clips does not sell, share or transfer any information to anyone. Videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails are sent only to YouTube, and only when you choose to upload them.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Press Disconnect in Church Clips: the app revokes its Google access and deletes the stored sign-in token from your Mac.
  • You can also remove Church Clips's access at any time from your Google Account security settings: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Stored YouTube information is deleted from the app within 7 days of access being revoked, and the app refreshes or deletes any stored YouTube data at least every 30 days.
  • Videos already uploaded stay on your YouTube channel until you delete them in YouTube Studio.
