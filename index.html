Church Clips

Church Clips is a personal Mac app. It finds each song in a recorded church service, cuts it into its own video, prepares a title, description and thumbnail, and, after the owner reviews and approves each one, uploads it to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Uploads use the YouTube API Services.

How it uses YouTube

  • The channel owner signs in with Google (OAuth 2.0) and grants permission to upload videos and read the channel's name.
  • Church Clips uploads only videos that the owner has reviewed and chosen to upload, with the title, description and thumbnail the owner approved.
  • It does not read, collect or analyze any other YouTube data, and it does not post comments, change playlists or act on other channels.

Who it's for

Church Clips is not a public service: it runs only on its owner's computer, for their own channel. There is no sign-up, no account system and no charge.

Church Clips is not made or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
